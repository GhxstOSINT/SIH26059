`sic_baseline.json` is a trained lag-regression research model consumed by the API. It was fit on Antarctic SIC from 2017-01-03 through 2020-08-06 and evaluated chronologically through 2022-12-31. Validation RMSE is 0.023802 SIC fraction versus 0.024319 for persistence; see the artifact for coefficients, counts, and caveats. The source is the paper's released testing set, so this experiment is not a reproduction of that paper and does not establish generalization to other periods/products.

`paper-convlstm/ConvLSTM.params` is a separate author-released Antarctic ConvLSTM checkpoint (CC BY 4.0; DOI 10.5281/zenodo.11234961). It is not loaded by the API. See its model card and the local data manifest for checksums and limitations. Keep model artifacts, source-data manifest, and evaluation report versioned together in an approved model registry before deployment.
