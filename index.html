About Southern Passage

Southern Passage brings voyage scenarios, dated sea-ice observations, and research evidence into one planning workspace.

The voyage map and route metrics are illustrative. The observed-data view summarizes a user-supplied line against a dated satellite snapshot. Neither view provides vessel-specific passability or navigation advice.
